# Advisory board

Last updated: 2026-11-19.

This page is the entrance for advisers to the operator's sites and repositories.

## Advisers to Sophaxis

Advisers to Sophaxis are sought through its [Advisory board: a call](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/advisory.md).
Advice on Sophaxis follows the rules of that page.
This page does not change those rules.
[Advisory board: a call](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/advisory.md) says: "The operator decides, and stays responsible for the site and its records."

## Advice on the other sites

Advice is also welcome on the operator's sites and repositories other than Sophaxis.
They are the repositories in the [Legal index](./index.en.md), and the sites built from them.
The scope of the advice is as follows.

- Advice is unpaid. An adviser pays nothing either.
- Advice is voluntary. An adviser can stop at any time by saying so.
- Advice is not employment. The operator makes no employment contract with an adviser.
- The operator sets neither the hours nor the place of an adviser's work.
- Advice decides nothing. The operator decides, and stays responsible for the outcome.
- Before being named, an adviser makes the declaration under "Conflicts of interest" below.
- An adviser speaks for themselves only. Advice does not involve the adviser's institution.

How the operator reads the Labour Standards Act and the Labour Contracts Act is in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md), section "Labour Standards Act and Labour Contracts Act".

## Who can join

Who can join is the same as in the Sophaxis [Advisory board: a call](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/advisory.md), section "Who can join".
The same words are used, so that the two calls do not disagree.
A check confirms that the list in this section is the list on the Sophaxis page.

- A researcher in philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics or the philosophy of science.
- Whose public ORCID record lists a journal article, a book chapter or a book with a DOI.
- Who does not live in, or stay in, a place where Sophaxis is not offered, as the [Submission Policy](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/policies/submission.md) lists them.

The age limits are the same as in the [Terms of collaboration](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/research-collaboration/blob/main/policies/terms.en.md) of research-collaboration.
An offer from a person under 16 years of age is not accepted.
An offer from a person under 18 years of age is accepted only with the consent of a parent or guardian.
How the operator reads the Civil Code is in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md), section "Civil Code".

## How to join

To advise Sophaxis, an offer is made as the Sophaxis [Advisory board: a call](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/advisory.md), section "How to join", sets out.
An offer to advise on the other sites can be sent by email to the same address.
The address is the email address on the operator's [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-1406-0547).
An offer gives an ORCID iD and a sentence on why the person would like to advise.
A person who prefers to make the offer in public can also use the [Advisory board](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/legal-and-governance/issues/new?template=advisory.yml) form.
That issue is public.
The operator decides whom to invite.
The operator aims to answer each offer within 7 days.
An adviser is named, with an ORCID iD and the declaration, under "Advisers" on this page, only after they agree to it.

## Conflicts of interest

The declaration is the same as in the Sophaxis [Advisory board: a call](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/advisory.md), section "Conflicts of interest".
Before being named, an adviser declares the following.

- Any position held now at a repository, publisher, journal, preprint server or index.
- Any payment received in the last three years from one of them.
- Any listed work of their own, or of a close collaborator, on Sophaxis.
- Anything else that a reader might reasonably see as a conflict, or that there is none.

[Advisory board: a call](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/advisory.md) says: "The declaration is updated whenever it changes. An adviser who declares nothing is not named."
The same applies to the advisers on this page.
An adviser does not advise on a case that concerns their own work, or the work of a close collaborator.

## Who runs the sites

Nobody runs these sites and repositories besides the operator.
The [operating rules](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/docs/governance.md), in Japanese, say: 「運営に使う時間は、週に 20 時間までです。」
[A call to researchers](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/call.md) of Sophaxis says: "The operator is at present the only moderator."
The conditions for joining and the declaration are set high on purpose.
The reason is to keep the offers within what the operator can check alone.
The conditions are not lowered because few offers arrive.
