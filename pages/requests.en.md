# Requests

Last updated: 2026-11-19.

This page says where a request about the operator's sites and repositories goes.
This page creates no new procedure.
Every request is handled under a procedure that already exists.
This page gives where each procedure is, and its main points.
Where the main points and the text of a procedure differ, the text of the procedure applies.

## Requests that cannot be made in public

A request that contains personal information is sent by email, not written in a public issue.
The address is the email address on the operator's [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-1406-0547).
The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md), section "Statements about people", says: "A request that contains personal information is received by email, not in a public issue."
The [Takedown Policy](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/policies/takedown.md) of Sophaxis says: "A request that cannot be made in public goes by email to the address on the operator's ORCID record."
This entrance creates no new contact.
The operator does not ask a requester to make their own contact details public.
A request received by email is answered by email.
The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md) says: "The operator's address is given without delay to a person whose personal information the operator has received, on request."

## The 10 repositories and deposit-check

The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md), section "Statements about people", receives errors in statements about people and infringements of their rights.
The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md) says: "An error in a statement about a person, or an infringement of their rights, is received at the email address on the operator's ORCID record, or in an issue of the repository."
The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md) says: "The operator checks each request promptly and corrects or removes the statement where needed."
The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md), section "Information", receives requests to disclose, correct, stop using or erase the personal information received.
Of those requests, the [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md) says: "No fee is charged."
The Disclaimer gives no procedure for a notice of an infringement that does not concern a statement about a person, such as copyright or a trademark.
The operator receives such a notice too, at the email address above and through the request form of this entrance.

## golden-window-resolution and this entrance

golden-window-resolution and this repository, legal-and-governance, are not named in the scope line of the Disclaimer.
For requests about these repositories too, the operator follows the same procedure as the [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md), sections "Statements about people" and "Information", and handles them in the same way.
How issues and emails to this entrance are handled is in [Personal data](./privacy.en.md).

## Errors in published works

An error in a published work is received through the "Report an error" form of its repository.
The forms are listed in the tables of the [Legal index](./index.en.md).
The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md), section "Checked intake", says: "From 2026-12-01 (Japan time), it applies to error report issues opened on or after that day."
The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md) says: "Errors that were found are kept, not deleted, in the errata and the record of corrections."
On main as of 2026-11-19, golden-window-resolution has no workflow for the checked intake.
The operator reads and handles the error reports of that repository.

## Records on Sophaxis

A request about a record on Sophaxis is handled under the policies and forms of Sophaxis.
The forms of this entrance are not meant for it.
The [Takedown Policy](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/policies/takedown.md) says: "When a removal issue reports exposure of personal data, the record page (its title, authors, abstract and PDF copy) is taken offline automatically, before the operator reads it."
That automatic step runs only on the removal form of Sophaxis.

## research-collaboration

A request about research-collaboration is handled under the procedure of its [Requests](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/research-collaboration/blob/main/policies/requests.en.md) page.
[That page](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/research-collaboration/blob/main/policies/requests.en.md) says: "The operator answers privately only when the requester has made a contact public on their own GitHub profile, and answers to that contact."
A request for a private answer without making a contact public is also received at the email address above.

## Requests received by the form of this entrance

The [Requests](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/legal-and-governance/issues/new?template=request.yml) form of this entrance receives requests that can be written in public.
The form asks which repository the request is about.
The operator handles each request under the procedure of that repository.
The form receives the following requests.

- Correction or removal of a statement about a person (without personal information)
- Notice of an infringement that does not concern a statement about a person
- Withdrawal of consent to giving an issue to a language model
- Another request for correction or removal

## What cannot be removed

The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md) says: "Earlier versions stay in the history of the public repository."
What others have already copied is outside the operator's reach.
