# Personal data

Last updated: 2026-11-19.

This page says how personal data received in the issues of this repository, legal-and-governance, and in emails to this entrance is handled.
The other repositories are covered by their own pages, listed in the [Legal index](./index.en.md).

## Who handles personal data

This repository is run by an individual, Takuya Nemoto (根本卓哉), the holder of the GitHub account cpsbvbng26-dotcom.
The operator's address is not published.
The address is given by email without delay to a person who asks.
How to ask is in [Requests](./requests.en.md), section "Requests that cannot be made in public".
How the operator reads the law is in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md), section "Act on the Protection of Personal Information".

## What is collected

This repository handles what is written in its GitHub issues and in emails received by the operator.
It handles the GitHub account name and what is written in the issue or the email.
The forms ask for no real name, address, affiliation or contact details.
Only the advisory board form asks for an ORCID iD.
No special care-required personal information, such as medical history or the state of a person's body or mind, is asked for.
This repository has no site, no cookies and no analytics.
Records made when GitHub's pages are viewed are handled by GitHub under its own policies.
Emails go to the same mailbox of the operator as those for Sophaxis.
The Sophaxis page [Operator and contact](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/contact.md) says: "An email reaches the operator's mailbox at iCloud Mail, which iTunes K.K., a company in Japan, provides to users in Japan, and which Apple Inc. may handle in the United States on its behalf."
How the operator reads the handling of that mailbox by companies outside Japan is in the Sophaxis [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/blob/main/policies/privacy.md), section "Services outside Japan".

## Purposes of use

- To answer requests for correction, removal and the like, and to handle them under the procedure of the repository concerned.
- To check error reports and keep them in the errata and the record of corrections.
- To read offers to advise, to check them against the public ORCID record, to answer them, and to choose advisers.
- To show an adviser's name, ORCID iD and declaration after the adviser agrees.

The operator uses personal data for no other purpose.
The forms made for this repository state these purposes before their first field.

## What becomes public

Issues are public.
They are put in a public place on GitHub by the person who writes them.
GitHub (United States) stores and shows issues under its terms and policies.
The GitHub Terms of Service allow GitHub and its affiliates to use content, issues included, to train AI features and models.
The phrase is quoted in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md), section "GitHub Terms of Service".
The operator cannot stop this use.

## Giving issues to a language model

The operator works on this repository with Claude Code.
Only for an issue whose consent box is ticked, the operator may give its title and text to Claude (Anthropic, United States).
The operator treats this as a provision of personal data to a third party in a foreign country under Article 28 of the Act on the Protection of Personal Information.
The English sentence of the consent box is "I consent to the operator giving the title and text of this issue to Claude (Anthropic, United States)."
On the forms made for this repository, the box is optional.
An issue without the box ticked is handled by the operator without giving it to Claude or any other language model.
The error report form is a copy of the canonical file in cpsbvbng26-dotcom, and its box is required.
Without consent on that form, an error can also be reported by email, to the address on the operator's [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-1406-0547).
Comments on issues and the content of emails are not given to a language model.
The consent can be withdrawn at any time, in the issue or by email.
After it is withdrawn, the issue is not given to a language model.
Issues opened by the operator are outside this rule.
The [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md), section "Issues and the language model", is written for the forms of the repositories in its scope.
The forms of this repository are handled as this page says.

## Other third parties

The operator gives personal data to no other third party.
A case based on law is an exception.
To check an offer to advise, the operator reads the public ORCID record of the person who made it.
Otherwise, the operator receives no personal data from third parties.

## How long data is kept

Issues stay until removal is requested.
An adviser's name and declaration are shown while the adviser serves.
When an adviser leaves, the name is taken off, and the date they left is kept.
Entries may remain in the history of the repository and in copies others have already made.
GitHub notifies accounts that are set to receive notifications of activity on an issue.
Depending on the reader's settings, a notification also arrives as an email that carries a copy of the content of the issue.
Deleting an issue does not necessarily remove copies in notifications already sent.

## Security measures

Besides giving the title and text of an issue with consent to Claude, the operator does not gather what is written in issues anywhere else and makes no copy of it.
Only the operator's GitHub account can change this repository.
Every change stays in the public history.

## If a leak or similar event occurs

If a leak of personal data or a similar event that the law requires to be reported occurs, the operator reports it to the Personal Information Protection Commission.
The person concerned is told in the issue they opened, or at the email address from which they wrote.
How the operator reads the law is in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md), section "If a leak or similar event occurs".

## Requests

Correction, removal, disclosure and stop of use of an entry about oneself can be requested.
There is no fee.
The procedure is in [Requests](./requests.en.md).
A request that contains personal information is received by email.
A complaint about how personal data is handled is received at the same address.
